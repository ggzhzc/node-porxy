What We Do

From planting trees to cleaning coastlines, our programs create measurable environmental impact while empowering local communities.

🌳

Reforestation

We restore degraded landscapes by planting native tree species. Each tree absorbs about 22 kg of CO₂ per year and provides habitat for wildlife.

Learn about tree planting →
♻️

Zero Waste Living

From cutting single-use plastics to proper recycling, we provide simple, actionable tools to help households and businesses reduce waste.

Get the zero waste guide →
🌊

Coastal Cleanups

Our monthly beach and river cleanups protect aquatic ecosystems. We mobilize volunteers and track waste data to advocate for policy change.

Join the next cleanup →
📚

Environmental Education

We bring hands-on sustainability workshops to schools and community centers, inspiring the next generation of environmental stewards.
